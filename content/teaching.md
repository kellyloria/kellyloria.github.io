# Teaching

My teaching practices focus on the interdisciplenary thenes used to in watershed science. I encourage my students to think about the connections between freshwater ecology, hydrologic processes, and ecosystem function. I emphasize hands-on learning, in class discussions, reproducible data analysis, and the use of environmental monitoring datasets to investigate additional aspects of watershed processes.

## Courses

### GPHS 782: Watershed Science Seminar
**University of Nevada, Reno | Fall 2026**
This graduate seminar explores watershed hydrology, ecosystem processes, and environmental change through scientific literature discussions, student-led research projects, and applied data analysis.

Topics include:
- Watershed hydrology and streamflow dynamics
- Snowpack, groundwater, and climate variability
- Remote sensing of vegetation and watershed conditions
- Stream water quality and ecosystem processes
- Environmental data analysis using R

**Course materials:** Course tutorials and reproducible workflows are available through my GitHub repositories. [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/kellyloria/GPHS_782_watershed_sci_seminar)
